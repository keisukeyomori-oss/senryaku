Original soundtrack policy

The shipped game loads five original 44.1 kHz stereo WAV loops from this folder:
  BD-01  title / town / story
  BD-02  battle / war
  BD-03  moonlit ruins
  BD-04  Hall of Lights headquarters
  BD-05  field journey

Six original effects live under SFX: select, move, attack, victory, defeat, and gift.
No third-party melody or recording is used. AudioManager retains a deterministic
runtime synthesizer only as a missing-file fallback.
